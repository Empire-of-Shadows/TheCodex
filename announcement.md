# The Codex just got an upgrade.

We've been rebuilding things behind the scenes, and a fresh wave of updates just landed. Here's what's new and what changed:

---

## Suggestions — Simplified

The old suggestion commands got consolidated. Less clutter, same power.

**Old** → **New**
- `/suggest submit` → `/suggest` — just type your idea directly, or pick a template
- `/suggest template` → `/suggest` — templates are now an option inside the same command
- `/suggest search` → `/suggest-search`
- `/suggest mine` → `/suggest-mine`

You can now submit suggestions as **free text or pick a guided template** — all from one command. Categories include Bot Feature, Server Improvement, Event Idea, Rule Change, and Other.

You'll also get a **DM notification** whenever the status of your suggestion changes (Approved, Under Review, Implemented, etc).

---

## Browse Prime Gaming Drops — `/drop`

**Brand new command.** You can now browse all the Prime Gaming drops and free game alerts we've posted — right from a slash command.

Use `/drop` to scroll through drops with pagination. See what's live, what's expiring soon, and what you might have missed.

---

## Welcome Messages — Revamped

New members now get a fully interactive welcome message when they join. It includes:

- Quick-access buttons for the **Server Guide**, **Getting Started**, **Free Drops**, and **Server Rules**
- A dropdown to explore **Server Info**, **Channel List**, **Roles**, or **submit a suggestion**
- A hidden easter egg (you'll know it when you see it)

If you've already joined — you didn't miss out, these features are all still available through their respective commands.

---

## Would You Rather — More Reliable

Daily WYR posts are now more consistent. We added safeguards so you won't see duplicate posts or missed days. The schedule runs smoother and threads still auto-create for discussion on every question.

Everything else you know — voting, stats (`/wyr stats`), leaderboards (`/wyr leaderboard`), and results (`/wyr results`) — works the same.

---

## Under the Hood

A bunch of behind-the-scenes improvements that keep things running better:
- Faster response times across the board
- Better timezone handling (no more off-by-one scheduling quirks)
- Improved permission checks so the bot asks for what it actually needs

---

That's it for now. If something feels off or you have ideas, drop a `/suggest` — that's literally what it's for.
